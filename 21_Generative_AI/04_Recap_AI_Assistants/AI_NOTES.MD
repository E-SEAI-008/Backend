# Chain of Principles for Responsible AI

## What Does This Diagram Show?

This diagram shows eight principles arranged as links in a chain. Each link is labeled with a value that appears to relate to responsible or ethical use of technology. The chain shape suggests that these principles are connected and work together.

## Key Takeaways

- **Proportionality and Do No Harm** is the first principle, focusing on avoiding harm.
- **Safety and security** and **Fairness and non-discrimination** are listed next, covering protection and equal treatment.
- **Right to privacy, and data protection** and **Sustainability** address personal data and long-term impact.
- **Human oversight and determination** and **Transparency and explainability** focus on people staying in control and understanding how systems work.
- **Responsibility and accountability**, **Awareness and literacy**, and **Multi-stakeholder and adaptive governance and collaboration** cover who is responsible, public understanding, and shared decision-making.

## Conclusion

Responsible use depends on many principles working together, like links in a chain. If one principle is weak, the whole chain can be affected.

# ChatGPT Confidence vs. Knowledge Curve

## What Does This Diagram Show?

This diagram shows how a person's confidence in ChatGPT changes as they learn more about how it works. Confidence starts high, drops after learning that ChatGPT is a language model, and rises again once the person understands where it is useful and where it can be wrong.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stage 1:** At first, ChatGPT seems amazing because its answers sound detailed, credible, and coherent.
- **Stage 2:** Learning that ChatGPT is a Large Language Model (LLM) lowers confidence. It is described as a statistical tool that predicts language without understanding it, producing "statistically plausible" answers.
- **Stage 3:** Confidence hits its lowest point when the person realizes ChatGPT can give incorrect answers by stitching together wrong pieces of information.
- **Stage 4:** ChatGPT is useful when there is no single "right answer," but it cannot be fully trusted when the answer must be perfect.
- **Stage 5:** A balanced view develops. ChatGPT can boost productivity, but users must recognize when it gets things completely wrong.

## Conclusion

Confidence in ChatGPT often goes up, down, and then settles at a more realistic level as people learn its strengths and limits. The main lesson is to use ChatGPT for productivity while checking its answers when accuracy matters.
